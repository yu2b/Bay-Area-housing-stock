Bay Area Housing Prices and the NASDAQ Composite, 2010–2025

Home to many major technology companies, the Bay Area has also experienced substantial housing-price growth. This visualization compares Bay Area housing prices with the technology-heavy NASDAQ Composite from 2010 to 2025 and explores how the post-pandemic housing recovery differed by county.

Hover over either line to view monthly values.

Sources: Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) and NASDAQ Composite (NASDAQCOM), FRED. Monthly data, 2010–2025.
